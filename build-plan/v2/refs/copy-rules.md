# Copy Rules & Vocabulary Standard

These rules apply across all merchant screens, buyer shop views, landing pages, and demo video script.

1. **Terminology:** Say "dispute", "buyer", "refund", "evidence". Never "case", "claimant", or "chargeback" unless referring to a specific external mechanism where that is the exact legal/protocol term.
2. **Action Clarity:** Every button specifies the concrete outcome: "Approve $50 refund", not "Submit" or "OK".
3. **Number Provenance:** Every number carries a currency and a source: e.g. "USD 107.50, from PayPal".
4. **Helper Placement:** Helper and explanatory text goes under the control, never hidden inside placeholder text.
5. **Brevity:** No sentence over 20 words on any screen a judge or merchant sees.
6. **Banned Words:** Never use: *seamless*, *effortless*, *unlock*, *supercharge*, *revolutionise*, *leverage*, *robust*, *cutting-edge*, *AI-powered* (as a descriptive marketing buzzword).
